# Photography checklist — NUTRIX LAB

Every image currently on the site is a marked placeholder. Replace them with real
photographs before the site is shown publicly.

## House rules

1. **One surface, one light.** Every ingredient plate is shot on the same surface,
   with the light from the same side, at the same distance. The point is that two
   plates can be compared, not that one plate looks good.
2. **Weigh things and say so.** The caption carries the weight and the preparation.
   A photograph of "some lentils" is decoration; a photograph of 120 g of weighed
   lentils is a record.
3. **Nothing staged as an experiment that isn't one.** Do not photograph glassware
   arranged to look like work in progress. Shoot the bench as it is actually set up,
   on a day when it is actually set up.
4. **People.** No identifiable children. For anything shot in a school, get
   permission in writing before the shutter, not after.
5. **Licensed images are allowed** for illustration, with attribution in the
   caption — but never captioned as this project's work, results or participants.
6. **Keep the raw files.** Crops change; originals shouldn't have to be re-shot.

## Technical

- Shoot RAW, deliver JPEG or WebP, longest edge ≥ 2000 px.
- Export at the crops above so the layout doesn't have to re-crop and lose the
  scale reference.
- Colour: the site's palette is sampled from lentils and red bell pepper. Keep
  white balance consistent between shots rather than grading each one to taste.
